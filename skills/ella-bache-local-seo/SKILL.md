# Ella Baché Hobart: Local SEO

Ella Baché Hobart is a salon at **7 Criterion Street, Hobart TAS 7000** (exact NAP in ella-bache-business). The Map Pack and Google Business Profile (GBP) are a steady source of bookings while organic web results get pushed down by ads and AI Overviews. Local visibility now has two layers:
- **Map Pack / Maps** - driven by relevance (category, services, profile, reviews), distance from the searcher, and prominence (reviews, links, web presence). Google's own help page names these three.
- **AI answers** - Google AI Mode/Overviews, **Ask Maps** (Gemini in Google Maps, live in Australia since 7 Aug 2026), Gemini, ChatGPT. They build answers from the profile, the website, reviews and third-party lists.
This skill owns both layers for the salon.

## 1. What moves rankings (evidence, Oct 2026)
Use this order when prioritising:
1. **Primary category** - top map pack factor (Whitespark 2026). Current: **Skin care clinic** (seen Sep 2026). Compare with Escape, White Sage and the other Ella Baché salons before changing; "Beauty salon" and "Facial spa" are the alternatives. Don't change without the owner's yes (and head office's, if they manage it).
2. **Open at time of search** - Google confirms "openness" is used; new #5 factor in 2026. Hours strategy matters (the salon closes at 4 pm Mondays and 2 pm Saturdays).
3. **Reviews** - Google says count and rating count. Localo (16,000+ profiles): review count is the biggest measured effect; rating acts as a threshold around **4.5**. Recency rose from #93 (2023) to #11 (2026). The salon was at 52 reviews, 4.4 (Sep 2026): below that threshold.
4. **Photos** - second-largest measured effect in Localo's data.
5. **GBP services** - Sterling Sky: predefined services now lift rankings for those terms; custom services help less.
6. **Website / salon page** - Google says organic position counts; ~93% of domains AI cites for local businesses are the businesses' own sites (BrightLocal). The profile's website link currently points to the head-office page (hobart.ellabache.com.au); asking head office to point it at ellabachehobart.com.au is in ella-bache-hobart-seo's head-office request pack.
7. **Links and citations** - prominence; citations are hygiene more than a differentiator.
What doesn't move rankings: **GBP posts** (Sterling Sky test, no measurable effect - they are a conversion tool), geotagged photos, social follower counts.

## 8. Google reviews

Reviews are a top map pack factor and the lever the salon controls most. The salon also has hundreds of reviews on My Local Salon (via Shortcuts); those can be quoted on the site, but they don't count on Google.

**Detailed reviews help** (Google shows matching snippets for searches like "facial Hobart", and Ask Maps reads review text), but they must come naturally. Never ask for specific content - no therapist names, treatments, keywords or star ratings. Give great service and an open ask ("We'd love your honest Google review"); clients mentioning a therapist or treatment on their own is fine. Photo reviews stay higher in "Most relevant" for longer.

**Getting more (compliant):**
1. Ask every client the same way, at the end of the appointment or at the desk - never only the happy ones. Past clients who never reviewed can get the same open ask, in small batches.
2. Desk QR card that opens the review link on the client's own phone (no shared salon tablet or kiosk).
3. SMS/email with the review link within 48 hours of the appointment (Shortcuts may automate this; check it doesn't filter by rating). Don't script what to write.
4. Ask genuine partners who have actually used the salon.
5. **No staff quotas, contests or rewards tied to reviews or name mentions** (banned 17 Apr 2026). Recognise great service in other ways.
6. Keep a steady weekly flow; gaps let rankings slip and spikes can trigger a 30+ day review block plus a "suspected fake reviews removed" badge. Formal season, Mother's Day and Christmas give natural volume.

**Rating and replies:**
- Target **4.5+** (from 4.4, Sep 2026). A rating in the 4.7-4.8 range with well-handled critical reviews often builds more trust than a perfect 5.0.
- Reply to every review **within 48 hours** in brand voice. Negative: acknowledge the issue in general terms, no arguing, no treatment or skin details, offer to resolve by phone or the salon email (from ella-bache-business). Under ~80 words, two drafts (warmer / more concise). Skin reactions or injury complaints: take offline, flag to the owner, and don't admit or deny anything in public.
- Recurring questions or complaints in reviews -> pass to ella-bache-hobart-seo as content gaps and to the salon page FAQ.

**Monitoring (monthly; quarterly deep dive):** reviews this month, total, rating, last review date, % with photos, reply rate; competitor table for the core searches (Escape, White Sage and the top 3 map pack names: total reviews, approx. per month, rating, last review, categories, hours); monthly review target to keep pace with or pass the leaders. Watch for unexplained drops - Google removes reviews without notice.

## 9. Review problems
- **Fake negative:** report each in GBP review management -> appeal if denied -> Google support -> GBP Help Community. "Never a client" alone isn't grounds; ex-employee reviews are removable if provable from public info.
- **Competitors' fake positives:** reportable (Google business conduct/redressal forms, ACCC) but rarely shifts rankings - invest in your own reviews instead.
- **Extortion / review attack** (flood of 1-stars then a payment demand): never pay; flag every review in GBP (and from a separate Google account in Maps); report the reviewer's profile in the Google Maps app; file Google's merchant extortion report with screenshots, timestamps, links. Often cleared in 24-48 hours; else contact support.
- **Missing reviews:** Google support (Reviews -> Review missing) with names, dates, screenshots; then GBP Help Community Product Expert. Also check our own requests weren't scripted (Google filters coached staff-name reviews).
- **After suspension/reinstatement:** wait a few days for resync; then support with profile ID, missing count, names, screenshots, original notification emails.
- Keep screenshots and monthly counts on file.

## 11. Measurement and drop diagnosis

**Sudden drop:** (1) check local SEO news for an update or bug (Search Engine Land, Search Engine Roundtable, Local Search Forum); (2) audit our listing (suspension, public edits, wrong hours, missing reviews); (3) check the market (new competitor, spam).
